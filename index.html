Pascu Keyword Research

A private tool operated by Marius Pascu (PFA), Timișoara, Romania

This is a private, single-user tool. After the owner signs in with Google and gives consent, it reads Google Ads data, such as keyword ideas and search-volume estimates, to support internal keyword research and business automation.

It is not offered to the public, has no other users and does not collect personal data from visitors to this website.
