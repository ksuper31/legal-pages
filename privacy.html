Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who we are

This application ("Pascu Keyword Research", the "App") is a private, single-user tool operated by Marius Pascu, a sole trader (PFA) based in Timișoara, Romania ("I", "me"). It is not offered to the public and has no customers or other users. It is used only by its owner to run internal keyword research and business automation.

2. What data the App accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and approves access, the App can read:

  • Google Ads data, such as keyword ideas and search-volume estimates returned by the Google Ads API (for example from Keyword Planner), and account and campaign information in the owner's own Google Ads account.
  • Basic account details that Google provides during sign-in, such as the account email address.

The App does not access the data of any other person. It does not collect personal data from visitors to this website, which has no forms, analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to research which search terms people use, so that I can plan and improve my own websites and services. It is not used to build profiles of individuals, for advertising to individuals, or for any purpose unrelated to that research.

4. Storage and security

  • Access tokens and results are stored only in the owner's own automation software (n8n) running on a server controlled by the owner, and in the owner's own spreadsheets and files.
  • Credentials are kept in that software's encrypted credential store and are never published or committed to source code.
  • Data is kept only as long as it is useful for the research, and is deleted when no longer needed.

5. Sharing

I do not sell, rent or transfer Google user data to third parties, and I do not share it with advertising platforms or data brokers. Data may be disclosed only if required by law.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

7. Your choices and rights

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), people whose personal data is processed have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro). Because the App handles only the owner's own data, these requests are not expected, but they will be answered if received.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by opening an issue on this site's GitHub repository (linked from the home page).
